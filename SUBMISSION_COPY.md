## Notes / Description

```text
VeriFlow Evidence Attestor is an independent GenLayer Intelligent Contract for reviewing public collateral-evidence pages through decentralized validator consensus.

A user submits a public HTTPS evidence URL and subject identifier. Validators independently retrieve the source and produce a structured VERIFIED, INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, or REJECTED decision. The contract stores a result only when validators agree on the decision and confidence remains within a defined range.

The project is designed as a reusable public-evidence review primitive for collateral-backed lending, treasury, or verification workflows. It does not process private KYC/AML data, determine legal ownership or value, move funds, or claim integration with any other project. The repository includes the contract source, review policy, public demo evidence, validation result, and deployment record.
```
